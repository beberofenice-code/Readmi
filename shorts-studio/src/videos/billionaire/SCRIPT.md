# "What If You Woke Up as a Billionaire?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s, ~205 words).
Premise: a regular guy wakes up worth a billion dollars and discovers money's side effect: nobody can afford to disagree with him anymore. The one honest voice left is the hot-dog vendor he used to buy lunch from.

Beats: hook (bedroom wake-up) -> butler + helicopter -> gold supercar + yacht -> 400 missed calls / cousin at the gate ->
boardroom yes-men + "sandwich of sandwiches" investment -> lampshade test -> whole board in lampshades ->
"You see" pivot -> night hot-dog cart, honest Sal -> million-dollar check -> payoff callback on "terrible".

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] The morning he wakes up a billionaire, everyone he knows starts lying to him.
[NARRATOR] His alarm clock is a butler with a silver tray, and his bed is bigger than his old apartment.
[BUTLER] "Good morning, sir. Your helicopter is warming up."
[NARRATOR] He buys a gold supercar before breakfast, and a yacht before lunch.
[NARRATOR] By dinner, his phone shows four hundred missed calls from cousins he has never met.
[COUSIN] "Remember me? Uncle Ray's wedding? I held your coat!"
[NARRATOR] Everyone laughs at his jokes now. Even the bad ones. Especially the bad ones.
[NARRATOR] His business idea, a sandwich made of two smaller sandwiches, gets a billion-dollar investment in eleven seconds.
[NARRATOR] So he tests it. He walks into the board meeting wearing a lampshade.
[NARRATOR] The next morning, the entire board is wearing lampshades.
[NARRATOR] You see, when you're worth a billion dollars, nobody can afford to disagree with you.
[NARRATOR] So late one night, he sneaks out in his old hoodie to the hot dog cart he used to love, and asks old Sal for an honest opinion.
[SAL] "Kid, you look terrible."
[NARRATOR] It's the nicest thing anyone has said to him all year, so he writes Sal a million-dollar check.
[NARRATOR] Sal squints at it and says his handwriting is terrible.
