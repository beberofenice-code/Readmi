# "What If You Think 100x Faster?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s).
Premise: only the mind speeds up 100x; body and world stay at 1x, so every real second is 100 felt seconds
(3 s greeting = 5 min, 1 h meeting = 4 days, 72 s red light = 2 h, 6 s laugh = 10 min, 7 min to fall asleep = 11 h,
80 years = 8,000 years, 3 min of toast = 5 h).

<!-- tts-tempo: boss=0.62 -->

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] The fastest mind on Earth spends most of its life waiting for toast.
[NARRATOR] One morning, Milo wakes up thinking one hundred times faster than everyone else.
[NARRATOR] At first, it's amazing. He reads an entire book while his coffee pours.
[NARRATOR] He beats a chess grandmaster in four seconds, and gets bored halfway through.
[NARRATOR] He solves a math problem nobody could crack for three hundred years, during a sneeze.
[NARRATOR] But then his boss walks in to say good morning.
[BOSS] "Good... morning... Milo."
[NARRATOR] To Milo, those three words take five minutes.
[NARRATOR] A one hour meeting feels like four days. A red light lasts two hours.
[NARRATOR] On a date, she laughs at his joke, and he waits ten minutes for the laugh to end.
[NARRATOR] You see, only his mind got faster. His legs, his mouth, and the whole world still move at normal speed.
[NARRATOR] So he's a genius trapped inside a statue.
[NARRATOR] At night, he can't even sleep it off, because falling asleep takes eleven hours.
[NARRATOR] Milo will live to eighty. To him, that's eight thousand years.
[NARRATOR] He has the fastest mind in history, and all he ever thinks is: hurry up.

## Beat Sheet
1. Hook — kitchen, Milo stares at the toaster.
2. Bedroom, morning — wakes up at x100.
3. Kitchen — book finished while the coffee stream hangs in the air.
4. Study — chess blitz, bored yawn; chalkboard proof during a sneeze.
5. Office — boss's slow-motion greeting (REAL vs MILO clock HUD), the endless meeting.
6. Street, golden hour — the two-hour red light.
7. Restaurant, night — the ten-minute laugh.
8. Pivot ("You see,") — street, frozen mid-stride; the statue on its pedestal.
9. Bedroom, night — wide awake.
10. Payoff — kitchen wall of tally marks; the toast finally pops on "hurry up".
