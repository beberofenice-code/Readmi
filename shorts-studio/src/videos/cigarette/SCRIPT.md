## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] Crunch! A rich man's cigar dies under a carriage wheel, and a barefoot beggar snatches it from the gutter.
[NARRATOR] Seville, Spain. Cigars cost a fortune, but the scraps are free.
[NARRATOR] So he shreds the leftover tobacco, rolls it in old paper, and sells it for pennies.
[NARRATOR] The poor can't get enough.
[NARRATOR] Soon, whole factories copy his trick: thousands of women, rolling by hand, four a minute.
[NARRATOR] Then, war. Soldiers in Crimea roll them in newspaper, and carry the habit home.
[NARRATOR] By eighteen seventy-five, one American tobacco boss is desperate. Hand rolling is too slow, so he offers seventy-five thousand dollars to anyone who can build a machine.
[NARRATOR] An eighteen-year-old farm kid calls him.
[BOSS:phone] "Eighteen? Kid, go back to school."
[JAMES] "Sure. Right after I take your money."
[NARRATOR] So in a leaky barn, James Bonsack builds it alone, while the rain pours.
[NARRATOR] One crank. The machine roars.
[NARRATOR] Two hundred every minute. A hundred and twenty thousand a day.
[NARRATOR] And the beggar's gutter trick became the deadliest product in history: the cigarette.
