# "The Elevator Stranger" — Master Script & Beat Sheet
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~190 words).
Premise: a candidate, late for the interview of his life, gets stuck in an elevator with a quiet stranger and
spends forty minutes telling her everything that is wrong with the company. She is the boss, and she stopped the elevator.

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] This man lands his dream job by insulting his future boss for forty minutes.
[NARRATOR] It's nine a.m., and Dan is late for the interview of his life, so he sprints into an elevator with one quiet stranger.
[NARRATOR] Between floors eleven and twelve, the elevator shudders, and stops.
[DAN] "No, no, no. Not today."
[NARRATOR] He pounds on the doors. Nothing. So he starts talking.
[NARRATOR] He says this company is a mess. The elevators break every week, the coffee tastes like regret, and the boss hasn't listened to anyone since nineteen ninety-eight.
[STRANGER] "So what would you fix first?"
[NARRATOR] And Dan, with nothing left to lose, tells her everything. Fire the consultants. Fix the elevators. And listen to the janitors, because they know where everything leaks.
[NARRATOR] Forty minutes later, the doors finally open on the top floor, and Dan runs to the interview room.
[NARRATOR] It's empty. Then the stranger walks in behind him, and sits down in the boss's chair.
[NARRATOR] You see, the elevator never broke. She pulls the emergency stop on every candidate, because a stuck elevator is the only place anyone tells her the truth.
[STRANGER] "You're hired. Start with the coffee."
