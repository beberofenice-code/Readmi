# "What If You Woke Up and Everyone Was Gone?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s, 186 words).
Premise: The last person on Earth gets everything for free, then learns that "everything" was other people.

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] You wake up one morning, and every other person on Earth is gone.
[NARRATOR] The street is silent. Cars sit in the road with their doors open, and nobody answers the phone.
[NARRATOR] At first, it's the best day of your life. No boss, no traffic, no lines.
[NARRATOR] You take a sports car you could never afford, and you fill a cart at the supermarket without paying a cent.
[NARRATOR] You move into the biggest mansion in town and give yourself a new job.
[YOU] "I declare myself king of everything."
[NARRATOR] But on day three, the lights go out, because nobody is left to run the power plant.
[NARRATOR] Then the taps run dry. Then the food on every shelf starts to rot.
[NARRATOR] So you climb the tallest radio tower and call out into the dark.
[YOU] "Hello? Is anybody out there?"
[NARRATOR] And the only thing that answers, night after night, is static.
[NARRATOR] You see, you were never doing it alone. Every light, every tap, every slice of bread was a thousand strangers quietly keeping you alive.
[NARRATOR] Now you own the whole world, and the only thing you want is a neighbor.
