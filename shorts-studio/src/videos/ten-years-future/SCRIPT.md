# "What If You Woke Up 10 Years in the Future?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet (~58 s @ 30 fps)
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s), second-person "what if" story.
Premise: You wake from a ten-year coma with a knee-length beard. The $200 you threw into a joke crypto ("PotatoCoin")
the night before your accident is now worth four million. You sprint out in your hospital gown, cash out at a robot
ATM... and the robot barista charges you four million dollars for a small coffee. Everyone else bought PotatoCoin too.
Voices (ElevenLabs): NARRATOR = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID, ROBOT = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID_ROBOT (Ava, female robot),
BARISTA = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID_BARISTA (Sigma Centauri, AI robot).

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] You wake up ten years in the future as a millionaire, and you're completely broke.
[NARRATOR] Your beard reaches your knees, and a robot is checking your pulse.
[ROBOT] "Welcome back. You have been asleep for ten years."
[NARRATOR] Outside the window, cars are flying, and drones are delivering pizza.
[NARRATOR] Then you remember: the night before your accident, you put your entire savings, two hundred dollars, into a joke crypto called PotatoCoin.
[NARRATOR] You check your phone. PotatoCoin is up two million percent.
[NARRATOR] Your two hundred dollars is now worth four million.
[NARRATOR] So you rip out your IV and sprint into the street in a hospital gown, beard flapping, dodging flying taxis.
[NARRATOR] At the first cash machine, you sell every single coin.
[NARRATOR] Bricks of cash pour out, and you hug them like a newborn baby.
[NARRATOR] Time to celebrate with the fanciest coffee in town.
[BARISTA] "One small coffee. That will be four million dollars."
[NARRATOR] You laugh. The robot doesn't.
[NARRATOR] You see, ten years ago, everybody else bought PotatoCoin too.
[NARRATOR] Now everyone's a millionaire, and money is basically worthless.
[NARRATOR] So you slept ten years to become a millionaire, and blew it all on one coffee.
