# "What If Your Shadow Walked Away?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s).
Premise: A man's shadow quits following him. It isn't running away; it is going where the light is.

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] One Monday morning, a man's shadow stands up, stretches, and walks away without him.
[NARRATOR] He's on the same gray walk to work he's made for eleven years, when it just peels itself off the sidewalk.
[MAN] "Hey! You can't leave. You're attached!"
[SHADOW] "Not anymore."
[NARRATOR] He chases it, but the shadow is faster. It doesn't have to carry a briefcase.
[NARRATOR] At the office, nobody notices it's gone. Under those lights, nobody there has much of a shadow anyway.
[NARRATOR] Then the postcards start arriving.
[NARRATOR] His shadow on a rollercoaster. His shadow learning to surf. His shadow dancing at a wedding it wasn't even invited to.
[NARRATOR] Everything he always said he'd do someday.
[NARRATOR] So he calls in sick, buys a ticket, and finds it on a beach at sunset.
[MAN] "Why did you leave me?"
[NARRATOR] You see, a shadow can only go where the light is, and he hadn't stood in the sun for years.
[SHADOW] "I didn't leave. I just got tired of waiting."
[NARRATOR] So the man kicks off his shoes and steps into the light, and his shadow slides right back to his feet.
[NARRATOR] It never ran away. It just went first, and for once, he was the one who followed.
