# "What If You Woke Up in a Zombie Apocalypse?" — Master Script & Beat Sheet (~60 s @ 30 fps)
Format: Shorts-Studio v2 vertical cartoon 3D short (1080x1920, 30 fps, ~3.7 words/s), second-person "what if" story.
Premise: You wake up to an empty street full of shuffling, groaning gray-skinned people and survive the "apocalypse"
movie-style (pan, pot helmet, barricade, dumpster) until the twist: it is the town's charity Zombie Walk you signed up for
and forgot, and your day of real survival grime wins you the trophy for Most Realistic Costume.
Voices (ElevenLabs): NARRATOR = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID, ZOMBIE = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID_ZOMBIE, HOST = ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID_HOST.

## Voice & Dialogue Script
[NARRATOR] You wake up in a zombie apocalypse, and somehow you're the only one panicking.
[NARRATOR] Your phone has forty missed calls, and the street outside your window is completely empty.
[NARRATOR] Then you see one: torn shirt, gray skin, dragging its leg right down the middle of the road.
[NARRATOR] So you do exactly what the movies taught you.
[NARRATOR] Frying pan in your hand, cooking pot on your head, and the couch shoved against the door.
[NARRATOR] But the fridge is empty, so you sneak out the back.
[NARRATOR] Another one lurches out from behind a car, arms out, moaning.
[ZOMBIE] "Braaains..."
[NARRATOR] You whack him with the pan as hard as you can.
[ZOMBIE] "Ow! Dude, what is wrong with you?"
[NARRATOR] Now there are dozens of them, all shuffling the same way, so you dive into a dumpster.
[NARRATOR] You climb out covered in ketchup, coffee grounds, and somebody's old bandages.
[NARRATOR] And you've stumbled right into the middle of the horde.
[NARRATOR] Every single one of them turns to stare, so you raise your pan for one last stand.
[NARRATOR] You see, last week you signed up for the town's charity zombie walk, and completely forgot.
[NARRATOR] Those forty missed calls were your friends, asking where you were.
[HOST] "And the prize for most realistic costume goes to... the guy with the pan!"
[NARRATOR] So you spent all day surviving the zombie apocalypse, and won a trophy for best zombie.
